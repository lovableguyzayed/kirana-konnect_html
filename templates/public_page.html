Kirana Konnect helps a shop owner keep bills, stock and udhar (credit) records. This page explains, in simple words, what information we keep and why.

What we collect

  • About the shop owner: name, mobile number, email (optional), shop name, GSTIN and Udyam number (optional), and your password (stored only in scrambled form).
  • Shop records you enter: products, prices, stock, bills, payments and udhar.
  • About your customers (entered by you): name, mobile number and address (optional), and what they bought or owe.
  • Camera: used only to scan barcodes on your phone. Photos are not saved or sent to us.

We do not collect Aadhaar numbers, contacts, location or SMS.

Why we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your account safe.
  • To make bills, invoices (PDF) and reports for your shop.
  • To show alerts for low stock, expiring items and pending udhar.

We do not sell your data and do not show ads.

Your customers' data

When you add a customer, you decide what to save and why. Please tell your customers that you keep their name, number and udhar record in this app, and delete it when they ask.

Where it is stored and for how long

Data is stored on secure servers and sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. We keep it while your account is active. When you delete your account, all your shop data is deleted. Keep your own copy of bills if the law needs you to (for example, GST records).

Your rights

  • See and correct your details in Profile.
  • Download a report of your data from Profile → Export business data.
  • Delete your account and all data: Delete account.
  • Ask a question or make a complaint to our grievance contact below.

Using the app

  • Kirana Konnect is a tool to record your shop's bills, stock and udhar. You are responsible for the records you enter and for the prices and taxes you charge.
  • Keep your password secret. Anyone who has it can see your shop's data.
  • Bills and reports are made from what you enter. Please check them before sharing.
  • Only add GST to bills if your shop is GST-registered.

Customer data

You collect your customers' details for your own shop. Only save what you need, keep it private, and delete it if a customer asks.

Payments and refunds

The app only records payments between you and your customers; no money passes through Kirana Konnect. The app is currently free to use. If paid plans are added later, the price, what you get and the refund rules will be shown before you pay.

Availability

We work to keep the service running, but it may sometimes be slow or unavailable. Download your report regularly to keep your own copy.

Closing your account

You can delete your account at any time from Delete account.
